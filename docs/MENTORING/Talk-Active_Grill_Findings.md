# Talk-Active — Grill-Me Findings

Source: `Preliminary_RistekHackathon2026_fam.pdf` (Team FAM, Ristek Hackathon 2026)
Session date: 2026-08-11

## Locked decisions

1. **SAM/SOM (Figure 6).** Keep SOM at 60,000. Doc must name the assumed
   10–15 partner-campus expansion driving it — UI alone only supports
   organic reach of ~3,000–6,000 over 3 years. Relabel SAM (~2.0M) as
   driven primarily by final-year defenders (~2.07M from 8.28M undergrads
   ÷ ~4-year program), not three additive segments.

2. **Campus tier pricing (Rp25–50M/year).** Anchor with cost-plus math:
   ~3,000 students × 3 sessions/yr × Rp1,500/session ≈ Rp13.5M raw
   inference cost × 2–3x margin ≈ Rp25–40M. Confirmed Campus tier bundles
   faculty-wide Pro-equivalent seats, not just the lecturer dashboard.
   Rp50M ceiling needs a bigger-faculty or premium-tier justification to
   hold.

3. **Innovation Week timing (real-world flag).** As of the grill session,
   the team was 2 days into the 5-day Innovation Week window (10–14 Aug
   2026), with 3 days remaining. Semantic evidence mapping — the single
   sprint objective — was not yet built. Fallback locked: ship with
   deterministic cue-matching and lean on the doc's existing "Honest
   boundary" framing if semantic mapping doesn't land in time.

4. **Rubric ingestion scope.** v1 limited to explicitly public rubrics
   (competition guidebooks, published scholarship criteria) — covers the
   competitor and applicant personas. The defender persona (private
   thesis examination forms) is deferred pending institutional buy-in,
   not a day-one MVP claim.

5. **Competitive moat (Appendix A).** Drop "accumulated Indonesian rubric
   archetype library" as a *current* moat — no real data exists yet at
   prototype stage with ~0 users. Reframe as execution speed + local
   Indonesian evaluation-context knowledge (LPDP formats, thesis
   structure, bilingual/code-mixed speech) as a near-term
   why-we'd-win argument, not a why-we're-uncopyable claim.

6. **Table 6 target metrics.** Reframed as hypotheses the Year 1 pilot
   (§8, 10-student study) is designed to test, not committed
   field-validated numbers. Add above Table 6:
   > "These are the hypotheses our Year 1 pilot (Section 8) is designed
   > to test, not field-validated numbers — we do not yet have outcome
   > data from real students."
   The 12-month funnel goals (10,000 registered / 5% paying, 3 faculty
   licences) remain as goals but should not be implied as evidence-derived.

7. **Pro-tier (Rp39k/month) willingness-to-pay.** Free tier (3 analysed
   sessions/month, uncapped duration) likely covers a full single-event
   prep cycle (persona's own 4–8 week window ≈ ~6 free sessions), so
   there's no natural forcing function to upgrade for most users.
   Decision: accept this — Pro converts mainly the "applicant" persona
   (parallel scholarship/job applications, higher session volume), not
   the general user base. Size the 5% paying-conversion target off that
   narrower segment rather than the whole registered-user base.

## Open item (not yet resolved)

- **Beachhead vs. revenue-driver misalignment.** Persona priority order
  (§2.2) ranks "the competitor" as beachhead #1, but Pro revenue comes
  mainly from "the applicant" (ranked #3). Current GTM plan (student
  orgs, competition communities) reaches the beachhead persona but not
  applicant-specific channels (e.g., LPDP prep groups, scholarship
  Telegram/Discord communities). Needs a decision: keep competitor as
  the acquisition beachhead but add an earlier, separate GTM motion into
  applicant channels — rather than waiting for the Campus-tier
  institutional phase to reach paying users.
