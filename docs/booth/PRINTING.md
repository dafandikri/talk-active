# Talk-Active booth printing instructions

## Files

- `output/pdf/Talk-Active_Booth_One-Pager_A4.pdf`
- `output/pdf/Talk-Active_Booth_QR_Card_A5.pdf`

## Print specification

### A4 one-pager

- Quantity: **30 minimum**, 40 preferred.
- Paper: 100–140 gsm matte white.
- Colour: full colour.
- Scale: **100% / actual size**. Do not use “fit to page”.
- Sides: single-sided.

### A5 QR card

- Quantity: **2**.
- Paper: 160–220 gsm matte, or mount on rigid backing.
- Colour: full colour.
- Scale: **100% / actual size**.
- Placement: one upright at the front edge of the table; one backup behind the laptop.

Glossy lamination can add glare and reduce scan reliability under venue lights. Prefer matte stock or matte lamination.

## Acceptance check after printing

1. Scan the A5 card with three different phones from approximately one metre.
2. Scan the one-pager from normal hand distance.
3. Confirm every device opens exactly `https://talk-active-id.vercel.app` without credentials.
4. Complete the full 390px judge path on at least one phone.
5. Record the results in [`BOOTH-RUNBOOK.md`](BOOTH-RUNBOOK.md).
