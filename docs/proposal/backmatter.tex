\section*{Appendix A --- Principal Risks and Mitigation}
\addcontentsline{toc}{section}{Appendix A --- Principal Risks and Mitigation}
% ============================================================================

{\fontsize{11}{14}\selectfont
\begin{tabularx}{\textwidth}{@{}L{4.6cm} X@{}}
\toprule
\textbf{Risk} & \textbf{Mitigation} \\
\midrule
The model misjudges whether a criterion is met &
Every verdict must cite a transcript span, and the student can override it. The
rubric parse is confirmed by the student before it is used. \\
\addlinespace
Indonesian and code-mixed speech transcribes poorly &
Pasting a written draft is always a first-class path, so the loop never depends
on speech-recognition quality. \\
\addlinespace
Students will not pay &
The core loop is free by design; revenue concentrates in the Campus tier, where
the budget holder is the faculty, not the student. \\
\addlinespace
A large incumbent adds a rubric feature &
Our moat is the Indonesian rubric archetype library and evaluation context,
accumulated locally rather than shipped as a feature. \\
\addlinespace
Privacy harm from unpublished academic work &
Private-by-default storage, no audio retention, explicit consent, and hard
deletion under UU PDP No.\ 27/2022. \\
\addlinespace
The team over-scopes during Innovation Week &
The MVP table fixes what is in and out. Semantic evidence mapping is the single
sprint objective; everything else already works. \\
\bottomrule
\end{tabularx}}
