## What changed today

Three defects the screens themselves revealed, all now fixed and pinned by tests.

**1. The workspace greeted every visitor by a teammate's name.** `index.html` hardcoded
`DF / Dafa / Personal workspace`. At the booth, a judge scanning the QR read someone
else's name as their own profile — a fabricated identity on the most-scrutinised surface.

Now: the chip renders from workspace state, defaulting to **Guest**. Clicking it opens
"Who is rehearsing?" — a name field plus *Continue as guest*. The kiosk reset clears the
name and re-opens the prompt, which is exactly the booth handover moment.

It is deliberately **not** an authentication system, and none of the copy says so.
`test/invariants.test.mjs` now fails the build on "sign in", "log in", "account",
"password" anywhere in a product surface — that vocabulary invites a judge's follow-up
question the build cannot answer. There is no cold-load name gate: a visitor arriving by
QR reaches the product immediately.

**2. Twenty seconds of an unchanging spinner.** A review runs a deterministic pass
(instant) and then a semantic pass (a network round trip, up to 22s). The user saw one
static "Mapping evidence…" label for all of it — indistinguishable from a hung page.

Now `src/analysis-progress.mjs` drives a two-stage panel:

```
✓ Cue matching complete        4 criteria checked on this device.
◐ Looking for the sentence      6s — falls back to the cue-matched
  behind each claim             result at 22s.
```

Everything shown is something the browser genuinely knows. There is **no** "criterion 2
of 4" — the client cannot observe that, and both the unit test and the browser check
fail the build if such a string ever appears. The advertised 22s deadline is asserted
equal to `DEFAULT_TOTAL_BUDGET_MS` in `src/semantic.mjs`, so the two cannot drift.

**3. The landing CTA was below the fold on the projector.** Measured, not guessed:

| Screen | "Start a practice" was at | now |
|---|---|---|
| 1440×900 laptop | bottom 821px | **666px** |
| 1280×720 projector | bottom 777px — *off screen* | **639px** |

Cause was `max-width: 14ch` on a 68-character headline, forcing five lines. `browser-check.mjs`
now asserts `ctaBottom <= viewport` at 1280×720 so it cannot regress.

## Frontend track — for the interface owner

Ordered by what a judge sees soonest. Each is independently shippable; `pnpm check`
green is your permission to merge.

**F-1 · Dashboard has two competing primary actions.** `Start practice` sits at y=135 in
the banner; `Continue practicing` — the one that resumes real work — sits at y=570, below
the fold at 720p. Pick one dominant action. The banner duplicates the `<h1>` and earns
little; collapsing it lifts the focus card above the fold on every screen.
*Done when:* one visually dominant CTA on `#homeView`, and a browser-check assertion that
the resume action's `bottom <= viewport` at 1280×720, mirroring the landing test.

**F-2 · Evidence review should make the quote the centrepiece.** The cited transcript span
is the entire differentiator and currently reads as body text beside a percentage. Make the
quote the largest element on the review screen. `--step-evidence` already exists in
`tokens.css` for exactly this.
*Done when:* the quote's computed font-size exceeds the coverage figure's, asserted in
`design-system.test.mjs`.

**F-3 · Social preview.** Sharing `https://talk-active-id.vercel.app` in any chat renders a
bare link — no title, no image. The team will share this at the booth. Add `og:title`,
`og:description`, `og:image`, `og:url`, `twitter:card` to `index.html` and `brief.html`.
Use an existing asset; do not add an external origin — `demo-gate.mjs` fails on any
resource loaded off our own origin, and that check is load-bearing.
*Done when:* tags present, `pnpm demo` still green.

**F-4 · Custom 404.** A mistyped URL currently shows Vercel's default page with Vercel
branding. Add `404.html` and register it in the build.
*Done when:* an unknown path serves our page.

**F-5 · `robots.txt` and a canonical tag.** Cheap, and the absence of both is on every
"this site was not finished" list. Three pages, so skip the sitemap — it earns nothing.

**F-6 · Four `<h1>` elements in `index.html`.** One per view. Only one is visible at a
time so it is defensible, but if F-1 touches the header anyway, collapse the hidden views
to `<h2>`. Low priority; do not open this on its own.

### Explicitly not on the frontend list

The launch checklist circulating online is written for local-business marketing sites.
Most of it does not apply here, and some of it would actively cost us points:

- **Reviews, testimonials, case studies, "trusted by" logos, team photos, customer
  counts** — we have no users yet. Manufacturing any of these is fabricated evidence in
  front of judges whose whole job is to check claims. This violates INV-1 and INV-2 and
  is the single worst thing on that list.
- **Google Analytics, cookie consent, Search Console** — analytics adds an external
  origin. `demo-gate.mjs` fails the build on exactly that, and `vercel.json` blocks it at
  the CSP. Deliberate, and worth saying out loud to a judge who asks.
- **Maps, opening hours, tap-to-call, service pages, payment terms, blog posts** — not a
  business site.
- **Custom domain instead of `vercel.app`** — a real consideration for a launched product,
  not for a hackathon exhibition where the QR is the entry point. Not worth a purchase and
  a DNS change inside the freeze window. Say so if asked; it is a deliberate choice, not
  an oversight.
